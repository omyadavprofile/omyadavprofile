## Brand icons
All brand marks come from **Simple Icons 13.21.0** (npm `simple-icons@13.21.0`), released under CC0 1.0 (SimpleIcons-LICENSE.md).
Version 13.21.0 is pinned on purpose: newer releases removed the LinkedIn and OpenAI marks.
Slugs used: react, nextdotjs, redux, javascript, tailwindcss, nodedotjs, express, mongodb, python, openai, n8n, make, git, github, wordpress, shopify, linkedin, instagram.
The marks remain trademarks of their owners and are used only to label the technologies and profiles they name.
The portfolio globe, arrows, pin, building and star glyphs are simple custom shapes drawn for this project.
